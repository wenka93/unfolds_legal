Unfolds Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

Ivan Borisov, the developer of Unfolds, provides this policy for the iPhone app and its widgets.

Data used by the app

Unfolds and its widgets work offline. They do not collect or transmit your personal data or usage data. The app has no accounts, ads, tracking, or analytics.

Unfolds stores your custom date ranges and preferences on your iPhone. The app and its widgets use this information to show your chosen periods and settings. Unfolds has no cloud sync. Depending on your settings, iOS may include app data in a device backup. You can manage iCloud backups in your iPhone settings. Apple’s guide to managing iCloud backups

Retention and deletion

To remove a saved range, open Custom Periods in Unfolds and choose Delete for that range. Unfolds keeps a local recovery copy in case it can’t read the main file. To remove the app’s local data, including that copy, choose Delete App on your iPhone. Manage any device backups separately. The developer has no server copy of your periods or preferences.
