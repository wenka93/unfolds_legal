Unfolds Support

Unfolds shows how much of a day, month, year, or custom date range has passed. It works offline on iPhone with iOS 18 or later.

Add a Home Screen widget

  1. Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen.
  2. Tap Edit, then Add Widget. Search for Unfolds.
  3. Choose a small, medium, or large widget and tap Add Widget.
  4. Touch and hold the widget and tap Edit Widget to choose its period, style and theme.

Add a Lock Screen widget

  1. Unlock your iPhone, then touch and hold the Lock Screen.
  2. Tap Customize, choose Lock Screen, and tap the widget area below the clock.
  3. Select Unfolds and add the rectangular widget. Tap it while customizing to choose its settings, then tap Done.

Show a custom date range

Open Unfolds and tap Period. If you already have a saved range, tap Add Period. Choose a start and end date, then tap Done. Both dates are included. Edit a widget and choose the saved range under Period.

If a widget says “Period Deleted”, edit it and select another period. iOS controls when widgets refresh. You can tap a widget to open its period and see the current percentage in the app.
